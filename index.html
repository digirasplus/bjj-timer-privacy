BJJ Round Timer

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-05-28 · Last Updated: 2026-07-03

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how BJJ Round Timer ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

The App is published by Digiras Software ("we"). This policy applies only to the BJJ Round Timer application; other Digiras Software products have their own privacy policies.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or account details. The App has no account or login, and we run no servers of our own.

The App does not:

  • Require an account, sign-up, or login
  • Request your name, email, phone number, or any other personal identifier
  • Access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone
  • Use social media SDKs or third-party analytics

To show rewarded ads, our advertising provider (Google AdMob) accesses your device's advertising identifier. This is the only information that leaves your device. See Section 4 for what is collected and how to opt out.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your timer settings: work duration, rest duration, number of rounds, prep countdown
  • Your preferences: sound, vibration, wake lock, round-end alarm sound, and color theme
  • A log of your last 10 sessions: date, duration, rounds completed, and tap count per session

This data is stored in your device's app sandbox. It never leaves your device. You can clear this data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the App, or
  • Clearing app data via Android system Settings → Apps → BJJ Round Timer → Storage → Clear data

4. Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob (a Google LLC service). Ads are optional and user-initiated: an ad plays only when you choose to unlock an optional premium color theme. The App has no banner ads and no interstitial ads, and no ad ever plays during a timer.

To serve and measure these ads, AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID) together with standard ad-request data (such as coarse location derived from your IP address and general device information). This advertising identifier is collected and shared with Google as our advertising provider. How Google processes this data is described here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (path varies by device), which limits ad personalization.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before serving personalized ads, using Google's consent mechanism (UMP). You can change your choice later.
  • If you never open a locked theme, no ad is requested.

Apart from AdMob, the App integrates no other third-party analytics, social media SDKs, or remote logging services. Fonts are bundled with the App; no external font requests are made after install.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads that use an advertising identifier, we recommend that children use it under adult supervision. If you believe a child has provided information or seen personalized ads and you have concerns, contact us (Section 8) and we will help.

6. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or modify on our end. To remove all locally stored app data:

  • Uninstall the App from your device, or
  • Clear App data via your device's system Settings

If you have any questions about your privacy rights, contact us using the information in Section 8.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document indicates the most recent revision. Significant changes will be announced in:

  • The App's Play Store changelog under "What's New"
  • The in-app footer in the next version release

Continued use of the App after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
